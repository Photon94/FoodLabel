FoodLabel uses libusb 1.0.29, dynamically linked under the GNU LGPL 2.1 or later.
The license is included in libusb-COPYING.txt.
Upstream source: https://github.com/libusb/libusb/releases/tag/v1.0.29
This local build bundles the Homebrew arm64 library, with only its install name changed.
FoodLabel source and rebuild instructions are supplied alongside the app. You may replace
Contents/Frameworks/libusb-1.0.0.dylib with a compatible library and locally re-sign the app.

NIIMBOT protocol reference: NiimBlueLib, https://github.com/MultiMote/niimbluelib
Reference revision: 932be70 (MIT license). FoodLabel contains an independent Swift
protocol implementation based on the documented packet formats and hardware checks.
