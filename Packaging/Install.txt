FoodLabel — macOS 15+ / Apple Silicon

Перетащите FoodLabel.app в Applications, затем откройте FoodLabel из папки «Программы».
Подключите Xprinter XP-365B по USB. Размер этикетки: 58 × 30 мм.
История печати и блюда сохраняются только на вашем Mac.

Drag FoodLabel.app into Applications, then launch FoodLabel from Applications.
Connect your Xprinter XP-365B by USB and load 58 × 30 mm labels.
Saved foods and print history stay on this Mac.

Updates preserve the database in ~/Library/Application Support/FoodLabel/.
Quit FoodLabel before replacing an older version. Use the in-app backup button first.

Signing and notarization status is stated in the GitHub release notes.
